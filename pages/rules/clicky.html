Game Rules

Clicky Wicky

  1. Choose rock, paper, or scissors to encounter computer.
  2. Winner are decided by classic rock, paper, scissors rules:
    • Rock beats Scissors
    • Scissors beats Paper
    • Paper beats Rock
  3. The player or computer gains 1 point for every round they win.
  4. No points are awarded if both choices are the same.
  5. The fastest to get 3 points will be the winner of the game.
  6. Have fun
