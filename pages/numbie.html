Numbie Wumbie

  1. The computer will secretly choose a number between 1-100.
  2. You enter a number as their guess.
  3. The system will tell you if your guess is too high or too low.
  4. You only have 5 tries to guess the correct number.
  5. If you successfully guess the number under 5 tries, you win the game.
  6. Have fun !
